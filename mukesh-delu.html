About

Dr. Mukesh Delu received the M.Sc. degree in Mathematics from the Indian Institute of Technology (IIT) Kanpur in 2021 and the Ph.D. degree from the Department of Mathematics, Malaviya National Institute of Technology (MNIT) Jaipur, in 2026. He is currently an Assistant Professor of Mathematics in the Department of College Education, Government of Rajasthan. He is a lifetime member of the Ramanujan Mathematical Society (RMS). His research interests include fractional calculus, medical image processing, machine learning and deep learning. His doctoral work on fractional-order kernels for retinal image analysis forms the basis of ChakshuRakshak AI.
