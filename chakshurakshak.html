Three analyses, one platform

A screening aid for ophthalmologists and diabetic eye-screening programmes. Pick the analysis you need; each runs its own model on the images you give it. Results are always reviewed by a qualified ophthalmologist.

Diabetic retinopathy

DR grading and segmentation

A five-stage diabetic retinopathy grade on the ICDR 0–4 scale (No DR to PDR) with a confidence score and referral advice.

  • Retinal vessel map and binary vessel segmentation
  • Lesion maps for microaneurysms, haemorrhages, hard and soft exudates (FLA-Net)
  • Both eyes or several images in one go
Diabetic macular edema

DME grading with GRACE-Net

Grades each image as no DME, or hard exudates away from or near the fovea, with the probability of each grade.

  • Hard-exudate overlay on the fundus image
  • Presence scores for MA, HE, EX and SE
  • Protocol-aware fusion of a direct and a grading-rule prediction, with an agreement check
Multi-disease · beta

Look-alike disease screen

A research preview that looks past diabetic retinopathy for conditions that can be mistaken for it.

  • AMD, myopic changes, retinal vein occlusion and chorioretinal scarring
  • Referable-DR and macular-edema probabilities
  • Overlays of DR lesions and other-disease signs

Clinical validation in progress

Real-world testing at SMS Hospital, Jaipur

The Directorate of Medical & Health Services, Government of Rajasthan (State Health Society, NCO) approved the pilot implementation and clinical validation of ChakshuRakshak AI for screening diabetic retinopathy and diabetic macular edema, in collaboration with the Department of Medical, Health & Family Welfare.

  • 1
    SMS Hospital, Jaipur · first two monthsFundus-camera screening with ChakshuRakshak AI and clinical validation against ophthalmologists. Running now.
  • 2
    District Hospital, Tonk · next phaseThe pilot continues at the district level after the SMS phase.

Weekly progress reports go to the State Directorate. Final diagnosis and treatment stay with qualified ophthalmologists; the AI assists screening.
