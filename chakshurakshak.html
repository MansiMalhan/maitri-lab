What it does

ChakshuRakshakAI-DR: an AI-driven vision protection framework leveraging fractional enhancement and vascular geometry for accurate diabetic retinopathy grading.

1

Fractional enhancement

Fractional-order filters sharpen vessels and small lesions such as microaneurysms before the network sees the image.

2

Vessel geometry

A segmented vessel map measures tortuosity and other vascular changes that accompany retinopathy.

3

Graded, explainable result

A 5-stage grade (No DR to Proliferative DR) with confidence, clinical indicators and a referral recommendation.

Clinical validation in progress

Real-world testing at SMS Hospital, Jaipur

The Directorate of Medical & Health Services, Government of Rajasthan (State Health Society, NCO) approved the pilot implementation and clinical validation of ChakshuRakshak AI for screening diabetic retinopathy and diabetic macular edema, in collaboration with the Department of Medical, Health & Family Welfare.

  • 1
    SMS Hospital, Jaipur · first two monthsFundus-camera screening with ChakshuRakshak AI and clinical validation against ophthalmologists. Running now.
  • 2
    District Hospital, Tonk · next phaseThe pilot continues at the district level after the SMS phase.

Weekly progress reports go to the State Directorate in alignment with the Madhu Netra AI programme. Final diagnosis and treatment stay with qualified ophthalmologists; the AI assists screening.
