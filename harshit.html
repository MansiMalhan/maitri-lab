About

Harshit is a PhD scholar in the Department of Mathematics at Malaviya National Institute of Technology (MNIT) Jaipur, working with Dr. Priyanka Harjule. His research is on the mathematical modelling of mosquito-borne disease, in particular dengue: hybrid integer and Caputo fractional-order transmission models, parameter optimisation against real-world data, climate-informed models with Bayesian decision support, and fractional physics-informed neural networks with ensemble learning for dengue forecasting. His work has appeared in Acta Tropica, Mathematics and Computers in Simulation and Procedia Computer Science, and at ICCS 2026 in Hamburg.
