About

Dr. Rinki Sharma received her master's degree from Motilal Nehru National Institute of Technology (MNNIT) Allahabad and the Ph.D. degree from the Department of Mathematics, Malaviya National Institute of Technology (MNIT) Jaipur, in 2026. She is currently an Assistant Professor of Mathematics in the Department of College Education, Government of Rajasthan. Her research develops fractional-order gradient methods for optimising neural networks and applies them to medical imaging, including deep learning frameworks for silicosis detection from chest X-rays.
