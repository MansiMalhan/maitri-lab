How it works

Given a retinal fundus image, ChakshuRakshak AI returns a severity grade aligned with the International Clinical Diabetic Retinopathy (ICDR) standard, together with a segmented vessel map, lesion-wise segmentation masks and a quantified lesion burden. What sets it apart is its mathematical core: instead of relying on a general-purpose convolutional backbone alone, it applies fractional-order operators at the enhancement and vessel-extraction stages, so that low-contrast early lesions and fine capillary geometry are preserved and carried forward into the grading decision.

What the system reports

  • DR grade on the ICDR 0–4 scale with model confidence and an explicit referral recommendation.
  • Stage-wise breakdown of the decision: detection → severity → fine grade.
  • Original fundus image alongside the extracted vessel map and binary vasculature.
  • Lesion-wise segmentation masks for MA, HE, EX and SE, with pixel counts and lesion burden as a percentage of image area.
  • About three seconds per image end to end, with a printable clinician-facing report.

Components of the pipeline

EnhancementFADHE

Fractional Anisotropic Diffusion–based Hybrid Enhancement: the pre-processing operator that suppresses noise while enhancing low-contrast lesion boundaries.

VesselsFHAU-Net

Fractional Hessian–based Attention U-Net for accurate retinal vessel extraction, producing the vessel map and binary vasculature used for geometric features.

LesionsFLA-Net

Lesion segmentation network that localises and quantifies microaneurysms (MA), haemorrhages (HE), hard exudates (EX) and soft exudates (SE).

GradingF2D-RetinaNet

Features-to-decisions dual-path framework forming the grading backbone, mapping enhanced image features and vascular-geometry descriptors to a robust retinal disease grade.

Clinical validation in progress

Real-world testing at SMS Hospital, Jaipur

The Directorate of Medical & Health Services, Government of Rajasthan (State Health Society, NCO) approved the pilot implementation and clinical validation of ChakshuRakshak AI for screening diabetic retinopathy and diabetic macular edema, in collaboration with the Department of Medical, Health & Family Welfare.

  • 1
    SMS Hospital, Jaipur · first two monthsFundus-camera screening with ChakshuRakshak AI and clinical validation against ophthalmologists. Running now.
  • 2
    District Hospital, Tonk · next phaseThe pilot continues at the district level after the SMS phase.

Weekly progress reports go to the State Directorate in alignment with the Madhu Netra AI programme. Final diagnosis and treatment stay with qualified ophthalmologists; the AI assists screening.
