SilicoShield AI
AI-assisted silicosis screening

Early detection saves lives

Silicosis is an incurable lung disease that affects stone, mining and quarry workers across Rajasthan. SilicoShield AI screens a chest X-ray for silicosis in seconds so that workers at risk can be referred early.

What it does

A screening aid for radiologists and occupational health camps. Results are always reviewed by a qualified radiologist or physician.

1

Fast screening

Upload one or many chest X-rays and get a result in under three seconds per image.

2

Research-backed

Built on the lab's silicosis research, including fractional-order gradient optimisers designed for training deep networks on small medical datasets.

3

Privacy first

Images travel over HTTPS, are processed in memory and are discarded after analysis. No medical images are stored.
