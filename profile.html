Brief Research Profile

Dr. Priyanka Harjule is a faculty member in the Department of Mathematics at Malaviya National Institute of Technology (MNIT) Jaipur. Her research lies at the intersection of Applied Mathematics, Machine Learning, Optimization and Fractional Calculus. She has authored over 30 research publications in reputed journals and international conferences. Her recent work includes fractional optimizers for deep neural networks and fractional-derivative filters for texture enhancement in medical imaging. Before MNIT she was Assistant Professor at IIIT Kota, where she founded and coordinated the e-Learning and Data Analytics Lab (e-LDA). She has led several government-sponsored research projects with a strong emphasis on interdisciplinary applications in engineering and healthcare.
